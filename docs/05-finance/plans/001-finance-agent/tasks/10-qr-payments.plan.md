# 001/10 — Pay invoices with a GiroCode QR: implementation plan

Task: [10-qr-payments.md](10-qr-payments.md). Spec: [README](../README.md), Decisions → "Payments leave through an EPC QR code".

**Goal:** the owner selects open invoices, picks an account, checks each payment card against its invoice, and pays by scanning a GiroCode in the bank app.

## Design

- **EPC payload** in `app/finance/epc.py`, pure: EPC069-12 version `002`, character set `1` (UTF-8), `SCT`; BIC optional; name cut to 70 characters; amount `EUR` plus two decimals, no thousands separator, 0.01 to 999999999.99; purpose and structured reference empty; the reference as unstructured remittance cut to 140 characters; trailing empty lines left out. Lines joined with LF.
- **QR** rendered server-side with `segno` (error correction M) as an SVG data URI. New dependency, justified in the commit.
- **Rules** in `app/finance/payments.py`, pure:
  - `draft_values(invoice)`: frozen payee, IBAN, BIC, amount, currency, and the reference: the invoice's requested reference, or its invoice number when it has none.
  - `skip_reason(invoice)`: `not_open` (paid or needs info) or `None`.
  - `approve_refusal(invoice, draft)`: `not_open`, `invalid_iban`, `iban_changed`, `amount_changed`, or `None`.
- **Table `payment_drafts`** (RLS): invoice (cascade), from-account (cascade), the frozen fields, status `draft` / `approved` / `completed` / `cancelled`, `approved_at`, timestamps. A partial unique index allows one `draft`-or-`approved` row per invoice.
- **Persisting** in `app/database/payments.py`:
  - `create_drafts(invoice_ids, account_id, user_id, mailbox_ids)`: the account must be the owner's and have an IBAN. Open invoices get a new draft and their old open draft is cancelled; the others are returned as skipped with the reason.
  - `approve(draft_id)`: re-checks the invoice, sets `approved`, and returns the EPC payload and the QR.
  - `cancel(draft_id)`.
  - Matching completes approved drafts when it links their invoice (auto or confirmed).
- **API:** `POST /finance/payment-drafts` (`invoiceIds`, `accountId`) → drafts and skipped; `POST /finance/payment-drafts/{id}/approve` → draft, payload, QR; `POST /finance/payment-drafts/{id}/cancel`.
- **UI:**
  - **Pay selected** on the invoice list asks for an account with an IBAN, creates drafts, and opens `/finance/payments?drafts=…`, which shows one card at a time ("2 of 5").
  - Each card: the amount largest, payee, IBAN, BIC, reference and the from-account, with the invoice PDF beside it, and a checkbox "I checked payee, IBAN, amount and reference against the invoice".
  - **Approve** is enabled only when ticked; it shows the QR and copy buttons. **Next** moves on, **Cancel** discards the draft.

## Steps

1. Red: `tests/finance/test_epc.py` (layout, truncation, amount format, BIC optional, range) and `tests/finance/test_payments.py` (reference fallback, skip, approve refusals). Green: `epc.py`, `payments.py`.
2. Model; migration autogenerated against the database with the owner's OK, RLS added by hand.
3. `app/database/payments.py` with mock-session tests: a second draft cancels the first; approve of a paid invoice is refused and changes nothing; matching completes approved drafts.
4. `segno` dependency; QR rendering test (SVG data URI).
5. API routes and route tests.
6. Frontend: Pay selected, payments page with cards; `pnpm tsc --noEmit`, `pnpm lint`.
7. Browser check of the tick and the QR; the owner's real payment check.
